# Roadmap

This sequence reflects dependencies and current implementation state; it is not a claim of completion.

1. **Foundation and quality gate** — active: finish typed repository boundaries, lint, migration coverage, and review closure.
2. **Onboarding and authentication** — next: premium first launch, saved progressive setup, Google Credential Manager. OAuth credentials are not configured.
3. **Dashboard and design system** — improve daily priorities and polish the local-data UI.
4. **Nutrition and food logging** — complete food CRUD, unit conversion, favorites, and provenance.
5. **Weekly retrospective logging** — bulk entry, duplication, missing-data completeness and weekly summaries.
6. **Recipes and meal planning** — recipe builder, scaling, schedule and saved meals.
7. **Grocery and inventory** — shopping lists, expiry and availability.
8. **Meal preparation** — templates, tasks and transactional inventory deductions.
9. **Workout tracking** — routines, sets, active session resume and history.
10. **Weight and body progress** — measurements, trends and encrypted local photos.
11. **Hydration** — history, edits and goal adherence.
12. **Automation** — task ranking, recurring plans, weekly reviews and adherence.
13. **Notifications** — just-in-time permission and local scheduling.
14. **Health Connect** — native activity read integration and permissions.
15. **Google Drive backup and encryption** — narrow scope, recovery, conflicts and restore.
16. **Performance and continuous verification** — Android metrics, Maestro coverage, CI emulator run.
17. **Final QA and release** — independent security review, release build, APK/AAB and setup docs.

Detailed task ownership and acceptance criteria live in [`TASKS.md`](TASKS.md). Dependencies and blockers must be kept current there.
